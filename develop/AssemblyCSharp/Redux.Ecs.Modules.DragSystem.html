    Class DragSystem

    Works out the drag and body lift of every flight part in air on each physics step, after the part modules ran and before the forces are written to the rigidbodies.

    Remarks

    The parts' velocities relative to their main body and the drag cube aerodynamics come from Burst jobs over the transform frames and the copied cubes. Each part used to work these out in Module_Drag through separate entity reads and writes, which made drag the largest cost of a large vessel in atmosphere.

    The main thread keeps what reads the part views or the managed curves. The velocity direction in part coordinates follows the part's view transform, and the drag curves are the same FloatCurve evaluations as before. The curves that depend on the Mach number alone are evaluated once per Mach number, which the parts of a vessel share.
