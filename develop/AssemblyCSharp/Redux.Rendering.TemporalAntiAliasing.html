    Class TemporalAntiAliasing

    Redux's temporal anti-aliasing, which the TAA setting runs in place of URP's own.

    Remarks

    The resolve is ReduxBetterAA's custom TAA by Michionlion (MIT), ported to URP in TemporalAntiAliasing.shader. It rebuilds the current frame from its jittered samples, takes motion from the nearest surface around each pixel, reprojects the history with a sharpened cubic filter and clips it against the neighbourhood in YCoCg. Depth agreement, motion and sudden changes of brightness lower the history weight, invalid motion falls back to reprojecting by the camera alone, and the background rotates with the camera without its translation. Sharpening applies to the output only, never to the history.

    It runs as a native-resolution URP upscaler, which gives it URP's jitter, the camera's colour, depth and motion, and a per-camera context for its history, and makes URP skip its own TAA pass. TemporalUpscaling selects it for the TAA setting while no upscaler is chosen, and UpscalerReactiveMask's mask lowers the history of transparent effects, which draw no motion of their own.
