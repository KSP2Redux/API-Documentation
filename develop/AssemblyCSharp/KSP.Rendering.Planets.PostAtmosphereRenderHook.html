    Class PostAtmosphereRenderHook

    Draws a bound local-space post atmosphere material on a camera, the way its technique drew in stock.

    Remarks

    Replaces the two Built-in pipeline paths. The Hillaire composite command buffer ran at CameraEvent.BeforeForwardAlpha, and the hook composites it before transparents. Stock's Bruneton post shader was a fullscreen quad in the Transparent-50 queue, after the water surfaces at Transparent-100 and before the cloud composite at Transparent-41, so Submit(Camera) draws it as a renderer in that queue slot, and for Bruneton the hook records nothing. Bind the material with BindPostAtmosphere(IAtmosphereRenderModel, Material, Vector3, Vector3, float, float, RenderTexture) first. Both shaders read _CameraDepthTexture, which URP copies for every camera the hook renders on because the hook asks for it. That includes the flight physics camera, which renders as an Overlay with its own cleared depth.
