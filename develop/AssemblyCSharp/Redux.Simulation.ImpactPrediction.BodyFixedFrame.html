    Struct BodyFixedFrame

    Converts a point on a trajectory into the place over the ground it will be at, as seen from the body.

    Namespace: Redux.Simulation.ImpactPrediction
    Assembly: Assembly-CSharp.dll
    Syntax
    public readonly struct BodyFixedFrame
    Remarks

    A line drawn in the body's rotating frame turns with the planet and ends on the landing site, where one drawn in the celestial frame drifts as the body turns. The conversion depends only on each point's time, and it is the one the forecast uses for the landing site itself.

    Remarks

    A drawn run is curved between its samples using the velocity as the tangent, so the tangent has to be the one of the converted curve. That is the turned velocity plus the part the turning itself adds, which is the derivative of the rotation applied to the converted position.
