    Class TerrainQuadErrorPass

    Measures the geometric error of newly meshed PQS quads on the GPU, from the working grid the stock quad kernel wrote.

    Inheritance
    object
    TerrainQuadErrorPass
    Namespace: Redux.Planets

    Syntax
    public class TerrainQuadErrorPass
    Remarks

    A quad's error is the largest second difference of its vertex heights along both grid axes and both diagonals, over the quad's own 9 by 9 vertices, divided by 8: how far a straight edge between vertices strays from a surface curving as the samples do. Results are read back without waiting and land a few frames later. They are recorded only by Flush(TerrainQuadErrors), so a job reading TerrainQuadErrors never sees them change under it.
