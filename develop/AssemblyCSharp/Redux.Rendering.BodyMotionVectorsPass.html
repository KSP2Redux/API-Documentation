    Class BodyMotionVectorsPass

    Gives the surfaces that move with the SOI body, the scaled bodies behind the physics scene and swaying vegetation their motion in URP's motion vectors, after URP's own pass.

    Remarks

    The physics space is an inertial frame, so the body's terrain, scatter and buildings move and turn through it every frame. URP reprojects every pixel as if it stood still in that frame and draws object motion only for renderers it tracked moving, which leaves out everything drawn by hand: the PQS terrain and the instanced scatter and vegetation. The pass reprojects the pixels that hold camera motion only as points fixed to the body, through the body's previous local-to-world times its current world-to-local. The matrix is taken in the same frame as URP's previous view projection, so a floating-origin snap needs no special case. Pixels at the physics far plane show scaled space. With ScaledSpaceMotionData from the scaled pass they are rebuilt from the scaled depth and carried back one frame with the scaled body they lie on, and the sky is turned back as a direction with the scaled camera, which also turns with a rotating body under a landed camera. Sky seen through a body's atmosphere beside it, the halo over its limb, is carried with that body instead. Pixels whose object motion a KSP2 MotionVectors pass drew are marked in the motion vector depth's stencil and left as they are. Last, while the frame is jittered for temporal anti-aliasing or an upscaler, the instanced vegetation draws its own motion with its wind at this and the previous frame's time (VegetationMotionDraws). The temporal SSR pass and the temporal upscalers read the result.

    PreviousFromCurrent(Matrix4x4, Matrix4x4)

    Computes previous times the inverse of current in double precision, which maps a point fixed to the body from where it is now to where it was.

    Declaration
    public static Matrix4x4 PreviousFromCurrent(Matrix4x4 previous, Matrix4x4 current)
    Parameters
    Type Name Description
    Matrix4x4 previous

    The body's local-to-world last frame.
    Matrix4x4 current

    The body's local-to-world this frame. Its 3x3 part must be invertible.
    Returns
    Type Description
    Matrix4x4

    The mapping, or the identity when current is singular.
    Remarks

    The 3x3 part is P times the inverse of C and the translation tp minus that times tc. Float inversion would lose centimetres to the body's distance from the origin, while the result moves points by a few metres.
