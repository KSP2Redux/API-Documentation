    Class TerrainShadowCascades

    Renders the shadow the SOI body's PQS terrain casts from the sun into three sun-facing cascades around the camera, which the main light samples everywhere (KSP2TerrainShadow.hlsl).

    Remarks

    Stock terrain cast no shadow: its caster never got its PQS matrix. URP's main light shadow map only reaches a few hundred metres, so crater lips and ridges need their own maps: a few kilometres, tens of kilometres and the visible horizon, the last growing with altitude. Terrain and sun hardly move in the body's frame, so each cascade is kept there (its shadow transform times the body's motion since it was rendered) and refreshed on its own interval, or early when the camera or the sun has moved. The caster is the stock terrain shadow material over every visible quad, so terrain off screen toward the sun still casts.
