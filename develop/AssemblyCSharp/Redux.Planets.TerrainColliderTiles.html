    Class TerrainColliderTiles

    Builds and keeps the terrain collider tiles of one body, from the heights the terrain shader draws.

    Inheritance
    object
    TerrainColliderTiles
    Namespace: Redux.Planets
    Assembly: Assembly-CSharp.dll
    Syntax
    public class TerrainColliderTiles
    Remarks

    A wanted tile asks the GPU for its vertex heights, turns them into a mesh with TerrainTileMeshJob, bakes it off the main thread and switches its collider on in the frame the bake finishes. A built tile nobody wants any more keeps its collider switched off, and is dropped least recently wanted first once the cache holds more than Redux.Planets.TerrainColliderTiles.CACHE_RETENTION times the wanted tiles or Redux.Planets.TerrainColliderTiles.MinCachedTiles, whichever is more, or passes Redux.Planets.TerrainColliderTiles.CacheCeilingBytes. Height requests whose results never arrive are made again after Redux.Planets.TerrainColliderTiles.RETRY_FRAMES frames. Dispose only after the body's GPU height requests are dropped, since a pending request writes into its tile's arrays.

    Remarks

    New must-have tiles are asked for at once. New prewarm tiles are asked for nearest predicted contact first, at most Redux.Planets.TerrainColliderTiles.PREWARM_REQUESTS_PER_UPDATE per call, and the rest wait for a later call. A tile asked for both ways counts as must-have.

    Remarks

    Switching a collider on builds its physics shape where its transform stands at that moment, and a later move only reaches the physics scene at the next sync. Placing first keeps a new collider's shape off wherever its pooled object last stood, which matters while time is paused and no physics step syncs anything.
