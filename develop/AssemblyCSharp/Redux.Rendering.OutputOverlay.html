    Returns
    Type Description
    TextureHandle

    A depth target at the colour's size holding the scene depth.
    Remarks

    After a temporal upscaler the colour is at the output size and the camera depth is not. The camera depth is copied once per camera frame, nearest texel, into a depth target at the colour's size. Map cameras always use a private depth/stencil copy for depth-writing gizmos and menu masks. Other cameras reuse matching scene depth or get a depth-only copy. Without the copy shader the target stays cleared and nothing occludes the overlays.
