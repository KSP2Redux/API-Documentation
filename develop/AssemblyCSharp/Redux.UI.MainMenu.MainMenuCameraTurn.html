    Class MainMenuCameraTurn

    Tracks how far the menu's camera has turned while the planet behind it is being changed.

    Inheritance
    object
    MainMenuCameraTurn
    Namespace: Redux.UI.MainMenu
    Assembly: Assembly-CSharp.dll
    Syntax
    public class MainMenuCameraTurn
    Remarks

    A change of planet is a turn rather than a fade, because a celestial body cannot be part drawn: its atmosphere shell is drawn or it is not, with nothing in between. Turning the camera rather than the bodies is what keeps the sun, the starfield and the planet moving together, since all three are only ever what the camera happens to be pointing at. It goes the whole way round rather than stopping halfway, so it finishes facing exactly what it faced to begin with and nothing in the scene has to be carried anywhere.
