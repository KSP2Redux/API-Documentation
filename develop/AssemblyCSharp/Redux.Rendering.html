    Namespace Redux.Rendering

    Classes

    BodyMotionVectorsPass

    Gives the surfaces that move with the SOI body, the scaled bodies behind the physics scene and swaying vegetation their motion in URP's motion vectors, after URP's own pass.

    CameraRenderEvents

    Per-camera render events with the Built-in pipeline Camera.onPreCull / onPostRender signature.

    CameraRenderHooks

    Registry of active ICameraRenderHook instances.

    CameraRenderHooksFeature

    Renderer feature that records every registered ICameraRenderHook for the cameras it accepts.

    CommandCameraRenderHook

    A camera render hook that records ordinary CommandBuffer commands against the camera targets.

    FfxUpscaler

    AMD FSR 3.1 and FSR 4 as a URP upscaler, through AMD's FidelityFX API and Redux's ReduxFfx native plugin.

    GalaxySkybox

    Draws the skybox the active galaxy declares wherever the stock game draws its galaxy cubemap.

    GameCameras

    Tells the game's scene cameras from the map view's camera.

    HdrOutput

    Switches the main display between SDR and HDR output.

    HeatRefractionRenderHook

    Draws the engine heat haze after the transparents, over a copy of the frame, the way stock's GrabPass did.

    ImageEffectRenderHook

    Runs a source-to-destination image effect on one camera, the URP replacement for OnRenderImage.

    LegacyFog

    Keeps the fog density KSP2's lighting profiles were authored for.

    LegacyLightCullingMask

    Restricts every light to the GameObject layers in its UnityEngine.Light.cullingMask, as the Built-in pipeline did.

    LegacyLightIntensity

    Keeps the Built-in pipeline light intensity and color semantics that every KSP2 light was authored for.

    LegacySkyboxReflection

    Gives scenes without a baked default reflection the skybox reflection the Built-in pipeline generated for them.

    LocalSunTint

    The atmosphere's colour on the local sun per pixel (KSP2LocalSunTint.hlsl): the tint stock gives the local sun at the camera, tabulated for the nearest atmosphere over the sun's elevation and the altitude.

    MapIconOverlay

    Draws the map view's icons and maneuver gizmos at output resolution after post-processing.

    MotionVectorDebugPass

    Debug views of URP's motion vectors on the flight camera, drawn over the colour before post-processing.

    OutputOverlay

    The scene depth and view-projection that IOutputOverlay hooks draw with.

    OverlayOpaqueTextureRequest

    Gives every on-screen overlay camera its own _CameraOpaqueTexture.

    PartMaterialLook

    Redux's look for bare metal on parts: more metallic and less smooth than the stock materials, so metal shows blurred reflections of its surroundings instead of a plastic sheen.

    PunctualShadowResolution

    Sizes the shadow map of every shadowed spot and point light by how much of the screen the light covers, as the Built-in pipeline did, and filters their shadows softly.

    ReactiveDlssUpscaler

    URP's DLSS upscaler with a bias-current-colour mask, so plumes, particles, clouds and water do not leave trails.

    ReactiveFsr2Upscaler

    URP's FSR 2 upscaler with a reactive mask, so plumes, particles, clouds and water do not leave trails.

    ScaledSpaceMotionData

    What the scaled-space pass hands to BodyMotionVectorsPass for one frame of its host camera, so the scaled bodies behind the physics scene get motion of their own.

    ScaledSpaceViewPass

    Draws scaled space at the start of the flight camera's frame: the skybox and every scaled-space renderer, culled and projected with the disabled scaled camera, into the flight camera's colour target.

    ScreenSpaceReflectionsPass

    Screen-space reflections on KSP2 G-buffer surfaces, traced against the current frame after the skybox.

    TemporalAntiAliasing

    Redux's temporal anti-aliasing, which the TAA setting runs in place of URP's own.

    TemporalHistoryCuts

    Drops the temporal history of the flight camera on a cut, so TAA, the temporal upscalers and the temporal SSR pass do not blend in the frames before it.

    TemporalUpscaling

    Selects URP's temporal upscaler (STP, DLSS or FSR 2, 3 or 4) and its render resolution for the whole pipeline.

    TerrainIceLook

    Redux's look for the icy lowlands of Minmus and Vall: their ice layer becomes near-mirror smooth, flatter and darker, so it reads as clear ice and reflects the sky, the vessel and the planets through the screen-space reflections.

    UpscalerReactiveMask

    Builds the reactive mask that ReactiveFsr2Upscaler and ReactiveDlssUpscaler pass to FSR 2 and DLSS, from what the transparent pass changed in the frame.

    UrpCameraStacker

    Rebuilds URP camera stacks every frame from camera depth, render target and clear flags.

    UrpQualityBridge

    Applies the game's QualitySettings shadow settings to a session copy of the URP asset.

    VegetationMotionDraws

    The instanced vegetation draws of this frame, kept so BodyMotionVectorsPass can draw the same instances again with their motion vector pass.

    Structs

    CameraRenderTargets

    The camera targets a CommandCameraRenderHook renders against.

    IOutputOverlay
