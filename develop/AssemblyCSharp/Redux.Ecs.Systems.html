    SimObjectDestroyEcbSystem

    Plays back the deferred destruction of simulation-object and transform entities once per frame, at the end of SimLateUpdateGroup - after both SimFixedUpdateGroup (FixedUpdate) and SimUpdateGroup (Update). Managed teardown still runs synchronously inside Destroy(). Only the entity destruction waits, so no structural change lands in the middle of a reentrant destroy. Playback cascades each entity's Unity.Entities.LinkedEntityGroup. The managed bridge cleanup pass then disposes the model's attached ObjectComponents and releases its handle.
