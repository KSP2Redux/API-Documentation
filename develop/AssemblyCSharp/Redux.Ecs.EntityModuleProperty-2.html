    Remarks

    While unbound (never-bound template instances, or after Unbind()), reads and writes use the inherited storedValue; Bind(EntityManager, Entity, Getter, Setter) seeds the component from it once.

    Bind(EntityManager, Entity, Getter, Setter)

    Points this property at entity's component and seeds the component's field from the unbound storedValue. The accessors are passed in (not assumed from construction) because deserialization can reconstruct this instance without running the field initializer that wires them. The generated binding always supplies them from its static delegates.
