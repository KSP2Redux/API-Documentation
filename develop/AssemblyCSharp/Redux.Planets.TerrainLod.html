    Class TerrainLod

    Decides PQS quad splits and merges by screen-space error, the screen size of a quad's geometric error.

    Remarks

    A quad splits when its error, seen from the nearest point of its bounds, covers more than the tolerance, or when its cells cover more than CELL_CAP pixels at that distance, which keeps flat ground from staying coarse. It never splits into cells that would cover fewer pixels than the floor at that distance.

    Every limit is measured as if the quad were seen side-on. Foreshortening against the body's radial direction read steep slopes turned toward the camera as edge-on, and a small camera move then flipped whole slopes between levels.

    Every limit merges at half of where it splits, so quads at a boundary do not flicker between levels.

    Detail also depends on the quad's TerrainLodRegion: full on screen, half in a GUARD_BAND_DEGREES band around the view and where a quad off screen casts a shadow into it. Elsewhere a quad neither splits nor merges by itself. It keeps its detail until the budget trades it away, first of all.
