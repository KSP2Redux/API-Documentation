    Class LegacyLightCullingMask

    Restricts every light to the GameObject layers in its UnityEngine.Light.cullingMask, as the Built-in pipeline did.

    Inheritance
    object
    LegacyLightCullingMask
    Namespace: Redux.Rendering
    Assembly: Ksp2Redux.dll
    Syntax
    public static class LegacyLightCullingMask
    Remarks

    URP ignores UnityEngine.Light.cullingMask per object. With rendering layers on in the URP asset, a light reaches a renderer only when the light's rendering layers and renderingLayerMask share a bit. Lights get their culling mask and renderers the bit of their GameObject layer, so the stock rigs (OAB parts and scenery lights, scaled, local and map view suns, kerbal face fill lights) light the layers they were built for.

    Rendering layer 0 is what a renderer or draw carries until something sets it: stock prefabs store 1, and new renderers and Graphics.Draw* calls take UnityEngine.Rendering.GraphicsSettings.defaultRenderingLayerMask. Every light keeps that bit, so a renderer the sync has not reached yet and an immediate-mode draw are lit by every light their camera sees, as before, rather than going dark. Command buffer draws get the default in the editor only: in players their rendering layer matches no light, so a lit shader drawn that way (the Decalicious decal pass) tests the lights against that bit itself. GameObject layer 0 moves to rendering layer 23, which is free because KSP2 leaves GameObject layer 23 unnamed and unused. A renderer on layer 23 gets bit 0. Rendering layer 31 marks the lights shaded as broad sources (SetBroadSource(Light, bool)) instead, so a renderer on GameObject layer 31 (Physx.Invisible, which holds none) gets bit 0 as well.

    Lights are mapped for each camera after culling, from its visible lights, before URP uploads their layer masks, so a changed culling mask applies from the next rendered camera on. Renderers are mapped when a scene loads, when the layer helpers Utils.SetLayerRecursive and GameObjectExtension.SetLayerRecursive relayer a hierarchy, through SyncHierarchy(GameObject) and SyncObject(GameObject) from code that renders right after it spawns or relayers something, and by a sweep over every loaded scene that checks a budget of renderers per frame and catches everything else within one pass. The budget drops once a whole pass found nothing to map and comes back with the first renderer that needs it.

    Runtime state only, in play mode. In edit mode nothing is mapped, so lights and renderers keep their stored rendering layers (bit 0) and every light reaches every renderer.

    Light light

    The light.
    bool broadSource

    Whether the light is shaded as a broad source.
    Remarks

    The KSP2 lighting shades a broad source as light arriving from most of the sky around a surface, such as the glow of the reentry plasma around a vessel, rather than from a point. A surface edge-on to the light is then still half lit, and fine normal detail does not switch between lit and dark at grazing angles.
