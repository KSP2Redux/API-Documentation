    Class LocalSpaceParking

    Parks loaded local-space views, the terrain sphere and everything spawned on it, across a universe reset.

    Inheritance
    object
    LocalSpaceParking

    public static class LocalSpaceParking
    Remarks

    A reset destroys every body view and the reload builds the planet again: the sphere restarts, the quads subdivide and the KSC statics respawn, which the game logs showed as seven of the twelve seconds a revert took. KSP1 keeps its planets through a scene switch. Here the view is detached from the body before the teardown reaches it, held inactive until the body that reloads it claims it, and handed back to that body, which re-points everything in the view that referenced the old universe. Only the current body is ever kept: KeepOnly(GameInstance, CelestialBodyComponent) drops every other parked view and releases every other body's local-space prefab as a body's local space starts loading, so a hop to another body rebuilds it and there is never more than one planet's terrain and bundles in memory. Scaled-space prefabs are bounded at the same point, to the bodies seen around the current one. Each parked sphere keeps its quad tree, height sampler and spawned statics, while its renderer has released its GPU buffers and colliders on deactivation. What it really pins is its planet's bundles, through the instance itself. Views nobody reclaims are dropped when the session ends.

    Remarks

    One body is parked at a time, and its scaled space goes with it: scaled-space prefabs stay only for the current body's neighbourhood that Redux.CelestialBody.LocalSpaceParking.IsScaledSpaceNeeded``1(``0,``0,System.Func{``0,``0},System.Func{``0,System.Boolean}) picks, the bodies the stock visibility pass loads scaled views for around it, so a revert to the same body still reloads all of them from memory. Every other body's scaled bundles used to stay for the session, and after a tour of the system their cloud cubemaps and masks pushed the VAB past the texture streaming budget (#2034). A released body whose scaled view is still alive keeps its bundles through the instance until the visibility pass unloads it.
