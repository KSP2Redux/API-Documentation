    Class SurfaceLandmark

    Authoring wrapper that coordinates a smoothing decal, prefab spawner, and discoverable entry at one lat/lon on a celestial body.

    Remarks

    Pure data at runtime. The SDK's editor-side sync helper reads these fields and reconciles the three managed children (PQSDecalInstance, ReduxSurfaceSpawner, ScienceRegionData entry) under the PQS. The wrapper GameObject itself is stripped from outgoing scenes by the build pre-process pass. If the strip is skipped for any reason the wrapper is harmless because it has no runtime behavior.

    Lat/lon/altitude are the source of truth for placement. The wrapper's Unity transform is a scene-view echo derived from these doubles by the sync helper, not the authoring surface.
