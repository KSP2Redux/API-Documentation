    Struct TerrainFootprintJob

    Works out the tiles a vessel could touch along its predicted path, as must-have and prewarm requests.

    Namespace: Redux.Planets
    Assembly: Assembly-CSharp.dll
    Syntax
    public struct TerrainFootprintJob
    Remarks

    The vessel moves along p(t) = v t + a t^2 / 2. Must-have discs cover the build time, each with its margin grown by aDelta t^2 / 2 with aDelta at ten g, for changes the prediction cannot see. Prewarm discs cover the rest of the horizon, a margin apart at the vessel's top speed.

    Each tile is requested once, by the first disc that reached it, so must-have wins and contact times are the earliest. Tiles already in Requested are left out.
