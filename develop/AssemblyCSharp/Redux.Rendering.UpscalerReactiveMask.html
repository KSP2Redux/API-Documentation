    Class UpscalerReactiveMask

    Builds the reactive mask that ReactiveFsr2Upscaler and ReactiveDlssUpscaler pass to FSR 2 and DLSS, from what the transparent pass changed in the frame.

    Remarks

    Plumes, particles, reentry effects, the cloud composite and water draw without motion vectors of their own, so a temporal upscaler moves their history with the opaque surface behind them and leaves trails. One hook copies the camera colour right before URP's transparent pass, after the full-frame passes that run ahead of it (post atmosphere, cloud raymarch, water background copy). A second hook after the transparent hooks compares the copy with the frame in KSP2ReactiveMask.compute and keeps the mask in the camera's frame data, where the upscalers read it. Both run only on the current game camera and only while IsActive is set.
