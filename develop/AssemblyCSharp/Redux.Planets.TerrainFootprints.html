    Class TerrainFootprints

    Works out, once per rendered frame, which terrain collider tiles each body needs under its vessels.

    Remarks

    Covers RigidBody, AtRest and Orbital vessels within the packed physics range. Each vessel's parts near the ground are covered along its predicted path: as must-have tiles over the time a tile takes to go live, and as prewarm tiles over at least the next three seconds. A vessel keeps its last tiles until it moves or turns by a meter, changes its parts, or has kept them for a second.
