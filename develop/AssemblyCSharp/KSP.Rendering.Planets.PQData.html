    Remarks

    Quaternion.FromToRotation leaves the rotation axis undefined when its arguments are antiparallel, so the engine picks one. The yNeg face normal is exactly (0, -1, 0) and is the only one of the six roots that hits that case. Unity 6.4 and earlier resolved it as 180 degrees about X, and Unity 6.5 resolves it as 180 degrees about Z, which yaws that face 180 degrees and points all four of its entries in the root adjacency table at the opposite face. Quads along the face seam then wait forever on a neighbour half a world away that never subdivides, so terrain in a band around the seam stalls at a coarse subdivision level and produces no colliders.

    Pinning the antiparallel case keeps the frame the adjacency table in CreateQuads(PQS) was authored against, rather than inheriting whichever axis the engine happens to choose.
