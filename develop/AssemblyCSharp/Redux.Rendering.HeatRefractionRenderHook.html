    Class HeatRefractionRenderHook

    Draws the engine heat haze after the transparents, over a copy of the frame, the way stock's GrabPass did.

    Remarks

    Stock's heat refraction shaders drew in the Overlay queue and read a GrabPass, so the haze refracted everything drawn before it: plumes, smoke, water and clouds included. Their URP ports tag their pass KSP2HeatRefraction, which URP's own passes skip. After URP's transparent pass this hook copies the camera colour into _HeatRefractionGrabTexture and draws every visible renderer with that pass on top. It runs on the current game camera, where engines are seen, and on SceneView cameras for the SDK previews. The copy costs a fullscreen blit on those cameras each frame, since URP no longer culls passes by empty renderer lists.
