    ModuleEntities

    Creates and tears down the per-instance entity that backs a single part module. Every module gets its own entity carrying its generated state component(s), config blob, the managed module object, and a ModulePart back-reference to the part. The part entity records its modules in a PartModuleRef buffer. Shared by the flight (PartComponent) and editor (ObjectAssemblyPart) binding paths.
