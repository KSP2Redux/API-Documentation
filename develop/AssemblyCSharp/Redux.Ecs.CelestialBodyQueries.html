    GetAltitudeFromRadius(in EntityManager, in Entity, in Entity, in Vector3d, out double)

    The altitude of localPosition (given in coordinateSystemEntity's frame) above body's surface, i.e. the distance from the body centre minus its radius. Returns 0 for a body that is missing or has no celestial-frame entity. The position is expressed relative to the body's celestial frame, so as a part's own transform entity is itself a frame, a part altitude is GetAltitudeFromRadius(body, em, partEntity, Vector3d.zero, out altitude).
