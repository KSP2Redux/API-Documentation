    Struct TerrainFootprintPartOrder

    A vessel's part spheres, ordered so each footprint disc tests only the parts low enough to reach the ground.

    Namespace: Redux.Planets
    Assembly: Assembly-CSharp.dll
    Syntax
    public struct TerrainFootprintPartOrder
    Remarks

    Every disc of one footprint moves the same parts by a displacement, so each part's height above the parts' centroid, less its radius and its sideways offset, is fixed for the whole footprint. The parts are sorted by that key once, and a disc stops at the first part whose key is past its threshold.

    The threshold is the disc's reach plus the ground radius less the centroid's distance, widened by three times the vessel's extent times how far up has turned with the displacement. A part past it is below no ground it could reach, and every part under it still takes the exact test, so the disc is the one testing every part gives.
