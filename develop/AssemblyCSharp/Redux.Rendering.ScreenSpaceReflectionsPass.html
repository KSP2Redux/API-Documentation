    Class ScreenSpaceReflectionsPass

    Screen-space reflections on KSP2 G-buffer surfaces, traced against the current frame after the skybox.

    Remarks

    The G-buffer pass writes the KSP2 environment specular into the lighting target. After the deferred lights, the forward-only opaques and the skybox, this pass builds a nearest-depth pyramid of the frame's depth (KSP2HiZ.compute) and a mip chain of its lit colour, traces one reflected ray per two by two pixels, resolves and blurs the reflection at half resolution, blends it with the previous frame's through URP's motion vectors, and swaps the environment specular for it by the trace's confidence (KSP2ScreenSpaceReflections.hlsl). The trace reads the current frame only, so floating-origin and Krakensbane shifts cannot smear what it finds. The history only steadies it, clipped to the current reflection around each texel. Transparents (water, clouds, the post atmosphere) are not in the reflections. It runs on the current game camera when that camera renders deferred, except in the map view (GameCameras).
