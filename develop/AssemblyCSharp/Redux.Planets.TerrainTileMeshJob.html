    Struct TerrainTileMeshJob

    Turns a tile's sampled heights into collider vertices relative to the tile's anchor.

    Namespace: Redux.Planets
    Assembly: Assembly-CSharp.dll
    Syntax
    public struct TerrainTileMeshJob
    Remarks

    Where the neighbor across an edge is coarser, the vertices between the neighbor's own move onto its edge, so the two meshes meet without cracks. Up to five levels of difference are stitched, the most a 32-cell edge can carry.
