    Remarks

    A craft used to be posed by copying whichever craft the scene had put in the same place, which is a poor source: the scene enables one of several at random, they are not all posed alike, and whichever one is copied has a spin of its own running through it. These are chosen instead, so a craft is shown at an attitude somebody picked rather than one it inherited.
