    Class LocalSunTint

    The atmosphere's colour on the local sun per pixel (KSP2LocalSunTint.hlsl): the tint stock gives the local sun at the camera, tabulated for the nearest atmosphere over the sun's elevation and the altitude.

    Inheritance
    object
    LocalSunTint

    Assembly: Ksp2Redux.dll
    Syntax
    public static class LocalSunTint
    Remarks

    Stock tints the local sun once for each atmosphere near the camera (AtmosphereDataModelComponent), so from orbit the surface at the terminator is lit white. Each of those atmospheres offers its tint here too. Before the frame renders, the nearest one's table and planet go to the shaders with the product of every tint the sun took, which the shaders divide out again.
