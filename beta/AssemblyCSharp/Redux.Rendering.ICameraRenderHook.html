    Interface ICameraRenderHook

    A piece of per-camera rendering that runs inside URP's render graph at a fixed point of the frame.

    Namespace: Redux.Rendering
    Assembly: Ksp2Redux.dll
    Syntax
    public interface ICameraRenderHook
    Remarks

    This replaces the Built-in pipeline pattern of attaching a CommandBuffer to a camera event. A system registers a hook with CameraRenderHooks while it is active, and CameraRenderHooksFeature records it for every camera it accepts.
