    Class GameCameras

    Tells the game's scene cameras from the map view's camera.

    Inheritance

    Remarks

    The map view (the flight map and the tracking station) renders the Map layer, which no scene camera renders. Its camera is the current game camera while the map is open. Passes that work on the physics or KSC scene, such as the screen-space reflections, skip it, since the scaled planets of the map reflect nothing on screen. The body motion vectors give it only the turn of its own sky: the body motion is a physics-space transform that means nothing for map-space pixels.
