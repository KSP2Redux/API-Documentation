    Class ReduxAutoExposure

    Histogram-based auto exposure, the URP counterpart of the Post Processing Stack v2 auto exposure effect.

    Remarks

    URP has no auto exposure. Redux.Rendering.PostProcessing.AutoExposureHook runs the package's histogram and exposure kernels on every post-processed camera whose volume stack enables this component, and multiplies the camera colour by the result before URP post-processing, where the package applied it. The parameters and their defaults are the package's. PostProcessingBridge fills it from the stock profiles.
