    Struct Module_Mine.GroundContactDebounce

    Counts how many consecutive updates the drill has failed to find ground under it, so a loss of contact has to persist before the mine reacts to it.

    The mine samples ground contact every fixed update, and part of that check reads vessel state that is written from the simulation on its own cadence. When timewarp ends and physics re-engages, a vessel resting on the surface reports a non-landed situation for a single update before settling back to landed. Acting on that one update switched a working drill off and left it off, since nothing turns the mine back on by itself (#1117).

    A genuine loss of contact holds across every following update, so waiting for TOLERATED_FAILURES of them costs a few tens of milliseconds of extra mining and rejects the transient. Free of game state so the sequence can be replayed in a test.
