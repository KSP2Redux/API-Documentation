    Remarks

    Backward Euler gives a strictly increasing residual for nonnegative conductance and radiation. Safeguarded Newton iterations retain a root bracket, including for enormous time steps. The iteration limit is independent of simulation speed. No destruction temperature is used as a clamp, so genuine heating remains capable of destroying a part.
