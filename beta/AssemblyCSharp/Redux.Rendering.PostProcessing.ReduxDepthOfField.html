    Class ReduxDepthOfField

    The Post Processing Stack v2 depth of field, rendered in place of URP's on cameras with a package layer.

    Remarks

    URP's bokeh mode descends from the package's but samples a different kernel (42 points on three rings, no centre sample, a 14 pixel cap) with a different circle of confusion, so it cannot give the package's image. Redux.Rendering.PostProcessing.DepthOfFieldHook runs the package's passes instead, before the auto exposure and bloom hooks, where the package ran them. The parameters, their defaults and their blending are the package's.
