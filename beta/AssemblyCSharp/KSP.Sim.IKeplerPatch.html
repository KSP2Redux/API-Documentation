    Interface IKeplerPatch

    Combined interface for a patched-conic orbit segment that carries full Keplerian solver state: patch bounds, encounter tracking, and closest-approach / SOI solvers. Both PatchedConicsOrbit and CurrentPatchedConicsOrbit implement this interface, allowing CurrentTrajectory to hold either.
