    Class TerrainLevels

    Chooses the finest PQS subdivision level a body draws, from the width its finest quads should reach.

    Inheritance

    Remarks

    A quad at a level spans the same share of a cube face on every body, so its size on the ground grows with the distance from the body center. The level is chosen at the outer surface radius, the radius plus the highest terrain, where quads are largest, so the width holds everywhere. Beyl's surface reaches four times its radius, and a fixed level drew it four times coarser at its peaks than at its base.
