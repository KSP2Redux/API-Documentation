    Struct NativeSpan<T>

    A non-owning view over a contiguous block of unmanaged memory: a raw pointer and a length. Unlike NativeArray it carries no safety handle or allocator, so it is blittable and can be stored inside an Unity.Entities.IComponentData or a blob asset and read from Burst. The caller owns the backing memory and must keep it alive for the span's lifetime.
