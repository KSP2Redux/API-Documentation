    Class ParkedViewLedger<TView>

    Holds view instances that outlive the universe they were built in, until the next universe claims them.

    Remarks

    A universe reset destroys every body view, and with it the planet's terrain sphere and the statics spawned on it, which the reload then rebuilds from scratch. KSP1 never does this: its scene switches keep the planet. This ledger is the bookkeeping behind parking those instances across a reset, and across a change of reference body. Each key holds at most one instance, a parked instance is handed out at most once, and an instance that died while parked is never handed out. Keys are reported oldest first.
