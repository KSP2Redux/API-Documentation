    Class TerrainTileLattice

    Lays terrain collider tiles out on the PQS cube-face lattice, every tile at the one depth whose cells are a PQS quad's at a chosen level.

    public class TerrainTileLattice
    Remarks

    A drawn PQS quad has 8 cells a side and a tile CELLS_PER_SIDE, so a tile at TileDepth, as many levels above the chosen level as that ratio's power of two, holds exactly the cells of the quads at that level under it. Collider vertices are then vertices of the drawn mesh wherever it is drawn at that level, on every body and at any height, since both scale with the distance from the body center alike. Not thread-safe.

    Vertices are numbered along X first, then row by row along Y. Face frames are exact signed axis permutations matching FaceRotation(Vector3), and directions are computed in double from integer lattice coordinates, so tiles sharing an edge get bit-identical directions on it, across face seams too.
