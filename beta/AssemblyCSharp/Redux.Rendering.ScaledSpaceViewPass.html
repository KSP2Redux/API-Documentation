    Class ScaledSpaceViewPass

    Draws scaled space at the start of the flight camera's frame: the skybox and every scaled-space renderer, culled and projected with the disabled scaled camera, into the flight camera's colour target.

    Remarks

    The Built-in pipeline showed scaled space with a camera of its own under the physics camera. URP pays its full per-camera cost (culling, render graph, shadows, volumes) for every camera, so the scaled camera stays only as the view definition (transform, field of view, clip planes, culling mask, skybox) and this pass draws that view inside the physics camera, one camera for both spaces. Scaled space keeps a depth buffer of its own. The physics camera's depth buffer and depth texture only ever hold physics-space geometry, as they did when the physics camera cleared depth over the scaled camera. URP sets the host camera's properties again right after the BeforeRendering passes, which restores the physics view after the scaled draws. The scaled depth, the scaled view and the motion of each scaled body go to BodyMotionVectorsPass through ScaledSpaceMotionData, so the temporal upscalers see the bodies move and turn.
