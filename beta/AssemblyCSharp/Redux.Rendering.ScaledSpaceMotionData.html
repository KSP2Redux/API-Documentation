    Class ScaledSpaceMotionData

    What the scaled-space pass hands to BodyMotionVectorsPass for one frame of its host camera, so the scaled bodies behind the physics scene get motion of their own.

    Inheritance
    object
    ContextItem
    ScaledSpaceMotionData
    Namespace: Redux.Rendering
    Assembly: Ksp2Redux.dll
    Syntax
    public sealed class ScaledSpaceMotionData : ContextItem
    Remarks

    The physics camera's depth holds only physics-space geometry, so every pixel at its far plane shows scaled space. Such a pixel is rebuilt from the scaled depth as a point in scaled space, carried back one frame with the motion of the body it lies on and seen through last frame's scaled view.
