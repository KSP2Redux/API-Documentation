    Class UrpCameraStacker

    Rebuilds URP camera stacks every frame from camera depth, render target and clear flags.

    Remarks

    KSP2 composites its views from several cameras that render in depth order into the same target, each one clearing only depth over the cameras below it (UI and helper cameras). URP only composites cameras through explicit Base and Overlay stacks, so this turns every depth-ordered group into a stack: a camera that clears color starts a new Base camera, and a camera that clears only depth, or nothing, becomes an Overlay of the Base camera below it. A stack whose whole viewport a later Base camera clears again would only be painted over, as the OAB camera clears over the star sphere camera and over the flight cameras outside Kerbin. Its Base camera becomes an Overlay in no stack, which URP does not render, so neither it nor its overlays nor their hooks run.
