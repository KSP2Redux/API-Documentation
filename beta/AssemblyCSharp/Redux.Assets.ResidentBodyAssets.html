    Class ResidentBodyAssets

    Keeps the celestial body prefabs resident across universe resets.

    Inheritance

    Remarks

    The PQS and scaled space prefabs are instantiated through Addressables and released whenever the universe is reset, which drops their bundles and makes every revert reload Kerbin and the KSC from disk. Retaining one asset load per key keeps the bundles in memory across resets. Scaled-space handles stay for the session. A PQS handle stays only while its body is the current one: KeepOnly(GameInstance, CelestialBodyComponent) releases the others as the next body loads. Everything left is released on the way back to the main menu, when the part data and templates go too.
