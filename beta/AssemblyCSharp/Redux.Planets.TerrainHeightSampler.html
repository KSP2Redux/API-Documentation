    Struct TerrainHeightSampler

    Samples terrain height on the CPU the way the bundled terrain compute shader does, quirks included.

    Namespace: Redux.Planets
    Assembly: Assembly-CSharp.dll
    Syntax
    public struct TerrainHeightSampler
    Remarks

    The terrain is drawn, and its colliders built, from the PQSQuadConstructor compute shader. It ships compiled, so its rules were measured by probing its SampleHeights kernel on Direct3D11. Together they reproduced the kernel to about 2 cm mean and under 1 m worst case on Kerbin, decals included. The stock CPU sampler, HeightSample(Vector2, Vector3, float, PQS, bool), is about 225 m off on average there.

    Each of these is reproduced deliberately, because the shader does it:

    1. UVs come from FXC's polynomial expansions of atan2 and asin, see GetShaderSphericalUVs(float3).
    2. Tiled maps blend a side projection at (2u, v) times the UV scale with a top projection at (x, z) / 2 times the UV scale, weighted by 1 - |y|^contrast toward the side.
    3. The global map and every tier sample biquadratically, whatever the body's filtering modes say, and both masks bilinearly. Only biquadratic has been verified, and every body checked uses it.
    4. An sRGB mask is decoded to linear before it is filtered, alpha excepted, as the hardware does. Minmus ships its layer mask that way.
    5. Layer mask weights are divided by their sum. A missing layer mask reads as black, which zeroes every tier.
    6. A tier channel's sub-zone weight is the dot product of the sub-zone mask with the channel's filter, neither normalized nor clamped, so overlapping zones stack. With sub-zones on it applies to all four tiers, and a black sub-zone mask leaves only the global map.
    7. Tiers 3 and 4 exist only with sub-zones on, and a missing map in either reads as white.
    8. Decal footprints are measured on the base sphere, the body radius less the ocean altitude, whatever the terrain height. Decals cull and clip at the body radius, so terrain height never clips one.
    9. Decal height and alpha are read through the low half of each packed texel pair, so every odd texel column repeats its even neighbour.
    10. No pole caps and no pole direction offset. The height kernel never reads the offset, and only applies a cap past a radius of 1,000.

    Burst-compatible. Every container has to be valid before the sampler goes into a job, which Build(HeightMapInfo, double, double, double, PQSDecalController, bool) guarantees.

    Remarks

    Marches in steps no longer than the ray could fall before meeting terrain of up to about 72 degrees, then bisects to 1 cm. Reports a miss once the ray leaves MaxTerrainRadius, passes maxDistance, or runs out of 2048 steps, which a ray skimming low over tens of kilometers of terrain can do. A ray starting under the terrain hits at its origin.
