    Remarks

    SRP Core's DLSSIUpscaler fills only the colour, depth and motion vector slots of the native texture table. This subclass records the same dispatch and adds UpscalerReactiveMask's mask as DLSS's bias-current-colour mask, which tells DLSS to trust the current frame over its history there. Context creation, jitter, resolution and mip bias are the base class's. It registers under its own name with the same DLSSOptions, and TemporalUpscaling selects it.
